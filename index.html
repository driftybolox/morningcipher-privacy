Morning Cipher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026
Developer: Cheshire SPL Studios
Privacy contact: morningcipher@gmail.com

About Morning Cipher

Morning Cipher is a family-oriented Android alarm app. It uses scheduled alarms and a camera-based QR-code challenge, with optional adventure themes, spoken messages and puzzle rewards.

Information stored on the device

The app stores the parent PIN, wake-up time, selected appearance preferences, narrator setting, and puzzle and alarm-dismissal statistics locally on the device. It does not require an app account or provide cloud syncing. The parent PIN is stored in standard app-local storage rather than a dedicated encrypted credential vault, so please use a unique PIN, not a password you use elsewhere.

Camera

The app requests access to the camera to scan QR codes on the printed cipher. QR-code recognition is performed on the device. Morning Cipher does not intentionally record, store or upload camera photos or video as part of scanning.

Alarms, notifications and audio

The app uses Android alarm scheduling, notifications, full-screen alarm notifications, audio and vibration to provide wake-up alarms while the phone is locked. Where Android permits, the app may temporarily adjust the alarm volume while an alarm is sounding and restore the previous alarm volume when dismissed.

Saving and sharing

When you choose to save an achievement picture, the app stores it in your device's media library. When you choose to share an achievement or cipher poster, you select a destination using Android's sharing features. The destination app or service handles shared material under its own privacy terms. Morning Cipher does not automatically add a child's name to achievement pictures.

Advertising, payments and analytics

The current release does not include an advertising network, in-app payment processing, an in-app user account or an intentionally integrated analytics service. Optional features such as seven-day premium scheduling or printed-poster ordering may be introduced later. We will revise this policy before introducing features that change data handling.

Children and families

Morning Cipher is designed for family use. A parent sets the parent PIN. The app does not request a child's name, email address or date of birth. Selecting a younger or older audience style changes the presentation only; it is not identity or age verification.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored app settings and puzzle statistics remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Copies of images or posters saved to your phone, and materials shared with other apps, may remain after uninstalling and must be deleted separately in the relevant apps or storage locations.

Security and third-party services

The app uses Android and third-party development libraries to deliver its functions. If you share material with another app, that third party's privacy practices apply. We will review and update this policy if the app introduces additional data collection or connected services.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or concerns, email morningcipher@gmail.com. We may revise this policy as Morning Cipher changes and will update the date at the top of this page.
